# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Fresh graduates aged 21–24 in Egypt, primarily using phones and often anxious about finding their first job.

## Product Purpose
Career Start AI helps graduates choose a career direction, learn through free roadmaps, prepare a CV, and practice interviews.

## Positioning
An Arabic-language graduate support platform combining five practical tools: an interest quiz, learning roadmaps, AI CV analyzer, CV builder, and interview practice.

## Operating Context
University graduation project with six team members and a two-week deadline. The target is a working, presentation-ready demo.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Three fields: programming, UI/UX, and graphic design. The quiz has ten questions. Roadmap resources are free. CV analysis uses Gemini through a serverless API and supports PDF upload. CV builder exports PDF. Personal and soft skills are coming soon. Preserve existing behavior, data, API contracts, and localStorage keys. Keep the existing vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript stack; no new libraries or build tools.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the existing logo and blue as the brand anchor. Voice is confident, warm, modern, and trustworthy; neither corporate nor childish. Arabic RTL is the primary experience.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing project pages, code, data, and logo are the source of product truth. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, or partner logos. Hero video files will be supplied by the owner.

## Product Principles
- Help graduates take one clear next step.
- Keep the experience comfortable on a phone.
- Preserve honest product claims and existing functionality.
- Make asynchronous states understandable and recoverable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Every theme must meet WCAG AA. Support RTL, keyboard use, reduced motion, and touch targets of at least 44px.
